## The bot webhook

Alpine sends a `POST` to your endpoint. The payload carries an `accessToken` the
bot uses to call back into the API, plus the `event`, a union of
<TypeLink name="BotWebhookEvent" /> variants.

## Payload

<Schema webhookPayload />

<Callout type="tip" title="Acknowledge fast">
    Return 200 immediately and process asynchronously. Alpine retries delivery
    until it receives a 200.
</Callout>
