## Create a space

A space is where your team's documents, tasks, and chats live. When you sign up, Alpine creates
your first space automatically — you can rename it any time from Space settings.

<Callout type="info" title="What's a space?">
    Think of a space as a workspace for one team or company. People, content, and permissions are
    all scoped to it.
</Callout>

## Your first document

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a document">Click <Kbd>+</Kbd> in the sidebar, or press <Kbd>C</Kbd> then <Kbd>D</Kbd> from anywhere.</Step>
<Step title="Give it a title">The first line is the title. Type "Q3 Planning" and press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>.</Step>
<Step title="Write with rich text">Use <Kbd>/</Kbd> to open the block menu — headings, tables, checklists, callouts, and more.</Step>
<Step title="Mention a teammate">Type <Kbd>@</Kbd> and pick a person to notify them and link their profile.</Step>
<Step title="Share it">Click Share in the top-right and choose who can view or edit.</Step>
</Steps>
