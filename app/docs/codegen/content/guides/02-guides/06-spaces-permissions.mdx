## Roles

Every account has a role within a space. Roles are hierarchical — an Owner can do everything a
Member can, and more.

| Role   | Can                                                |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner  | Manage billing, delete the space, everything below |
| Admin  | Manage people, bots, and integrations              |
| Member | Create and collaborate on content                  |

<Callout type="warning" title="Removing people">
    Removed accounts become inactive rather than deleted, so their past contributions stay
    attributed and linkable.
</Callout>
