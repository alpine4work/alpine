AI feels most natural when it can see the same trail of work that people see.
Not an exported snapshot. Not a pasted bundle of context. The actual document,
task, thread, and decision history.

## Context beats ceremony

The collaboration model matters more than the prompt box. If an agent can leave
a comment, draft a change, summarize the current thread, or open a task with the
right references attached, it starts to feel like a teammate with good habits.

That is the product direction we care about: agents that reduce ceremony without
removing accountability.
