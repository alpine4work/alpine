The easiest productivity tool to build is one that accepts every notification,
every mention, and every update with the same volume.

The better one makes attention a first-class product surface. It knows when a
document edit matters, when a task comment is just bookkeeping, and when a quiet
summary beats another red badge.

## Useful defaults

Alpine is designed around a simple loop: gather the work, preserve its context,
and surface the next useful thing. That means search, inbox, and feeds have to
share vocabulary instead of behaving like separate products.

![A diagram of Alpine work surfaces](/blog/images/editor-pattern.svg)

When those surfaces agree, teams spend less time reassembling what happened and
more time deciding what happens next.
