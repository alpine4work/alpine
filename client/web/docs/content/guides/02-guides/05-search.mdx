## Using search

Press <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> anywhere to open search. Results are ranked and grouped by type,
with a live preview snippet. Alpine also learns what you work with — people and documents you
touch often rank higher.

## Suggestions

Before you type anything, search suggests recent and frequently used items so jumping back into
your work is one keystroke away.
