## Channels & posts

A channel holds posts; a post holds a threaded conversation. Posts appear newest first, and every
post supports the full rich text editor — headings, tables, files, and mentions.

<Callout type="tip" title="Feed vs chat">
    Use channels for announcements and durable discussion; use chat for fast back-and-forth.
</Callout>
